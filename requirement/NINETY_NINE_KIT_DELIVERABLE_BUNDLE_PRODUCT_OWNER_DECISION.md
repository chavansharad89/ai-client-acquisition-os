## 6. DECIDED — Decision C: ₹99/higher-tier boundaries

### Candidate #6 — Opportunity qualification checklist: APPROVE, narrow framing
**DECIDED.** Scope is fixed, narrowly, to the single DISCOVER-level question: *"Is this prospect worth a first message?"* It **must not** become a detailed project-scoping, requirements-gathering, pricing, or proposal-qualification system — that remains the ₹499 tier's territory, undisturbed by this decision.

### Candidate #7 — Simple prospect scorecard: APPROVE, manual/static framing
**DECIDED.** Kept strictly as a manual/static, downloadable prioritization exercise. It **must not** be represented, marketed, or framed as equivalent to the automated higher-tier scoring engine. The higher tier's automated scoring capability remains differentiated and undiminished by this decision.

### Candidate #9 — Follow-up sequence templates: REJECT
**DECIDED.** Rejected outright. No reduced or "one-touch" alternative version is to be created as a substitute. The existing ₹99 outreach assets (asset #3) are deemed sufficient for launch. The fuller follow-up sequence remains exclusively differentiated for the ₹499 tier.
