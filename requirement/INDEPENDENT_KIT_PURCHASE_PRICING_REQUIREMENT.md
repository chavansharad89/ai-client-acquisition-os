# Independent Kit Purchase & Upgrade Pricing — Requirement

## 1. Summary

The product shall allow a customer to purchase any available kit directly, without requiring purchase of a
lower-priced kit first. A later purchase of a higher-priced kit is a new, full-price purchase — not an upgrade
that credits or discounts prior purchases.

## 2. Pricing

The currently defined kits are:

- ₹99 Kit — independently purchasable at ₹99.
- ₹499 Kit — independently purchasable at ₹499.
- ₹1,499 Kit — independently purchasable at ₹1,499.

## 3. Purchase rules

1. A customer may purchase the ₹99 Kit directly.
2. A customer may purchase the ₹499 Kit directly, without previously purchasing the ₹99 Kit.
3. A customer may purchase the ₹1,499 Kit directly, without previously purchasing either the ₹99 or ₹499 Kit.
4. Purchasing a lower-priced kit shall not be a prerequisite for purchasing a higher-priced kit.
5. Purchasing a lower-priced kit shall not create any monetary credit, discount, balance, or price offset toward
   a subsequent higher-priced kit.
6. If a customer who previously purchased the ₹99 Kit subsequently purchases the ₹499 Kit, the customer shall pay
   the full ₹499 price.
7. If a customer who previously purchased the ₹99 Kit subsequently purchases the ₹1,499 Kit, the customer shall
   pay the full ₹1,499 price.
8. If a customer who previously purchased the ₹499 Kit subsequently purchases the ₹1,499 Kit, the customer shall
   pay the full ₹1,499 price.
9. A customer who purchases all three kits shall therefore pay a cumulative ₹2,097.
10. The system shall not automatically apply the price of previously purchased kits as a discount against a
    subsequent purchase.

## 4. UX / positioning

- The three kits shall be presented as independently purchasable products, not as mandatory stages of a
  sequential funnel.
- The product experience may describe a subsequent purchase as an upgrade for an existing customer, but the
  upgrade shall remain a new purchase at the full listed price.
- The customer shall always have the option to select the highest-priced kit directly.

## 5. Example

A new customer who wants the complete solution may select:

> ₹1,499 Kit → Pay ₹1,499

They shall not be required to first purchase:

> ₹99 Kit → ₹499 Kit → ₹1,499 Kit

A customer who instead starts with the ₹99 Kit may later purchase the ₹499 or ₹1,499 Kit, but the previous
purchase shall not reduce the subsequent purchase price.
