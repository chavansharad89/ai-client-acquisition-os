## A. AUTHORITATIVE COMMERCIAL DECISIONS (COMMERCIAL-PO-01 .. 07)

These were handed to the analyst directly by the Product Owner in this governance request (not previously present anywhere in the repository). This document is their first recorded location. Wording is preserved verbatim below; only light re-indentation was applied.

> **COMMERCIAL-PO-01 — Three product subscriptions.** The product family consists of: (1) ₹99 kit, (2) ₹499 kit, (3) ₹1,499 Client Finder. Each is represented as a subscription/product entitlement with its own lifecycle. The ₹99 and ₹499 products may be purchased independently. The ₹1,499 Client Finder is the complete-package subscription. Each subscription period is 30 days.
>
> **COMMERCIAL-PO-02 — ₹1,499 complete-package access.** An active ₹1,499 Client Finder subscription grants access to ₹99 kit + ₹499 kit + ₹1,499 Client Finder. No existing ₹99 or ₹499 subscription is required to purchase ₹1,499; a customer can directly purchase ₹1,499 and receive all three package accesses. However, ₹99 ownership does NOT automatically grant ₹1,499 access; ₹499 ownership does NOT automatically grant ₹1,499 access; ₹99+₹499 ownership does NOT automatically grant ₹1,499 access. A customer who wants Client Finder access must purchase ₹1,499.
>
> **COMMERCIAL-PO-03 — Independent subscriptions + complete-package access.** ₹99, ₹499, and ₹1,499 have independent subscription/payment lifecycles. Buying ₹1,499 does NOT cancel, replace, or merge ₹99/₹499, nor modify their existing lifecycle. Per the PO's worked examples: buying ₹1,499 while ₹99 (and/or ₹499) is active leaves all subscriptions active independently, with ₹499/₹99 access additionally covered by the ₹1,499 complete-package entitlement; cancelling ₹1,499 does not cancel ₹99/₹499; cancelling ₹99 does not cancel ₹499/₹1,499; cancelling ₹499 does not cancel ₹99/₹1,499. Each separately purchased subscription follows its own renewal/cancellation lifecycle.
>
> **COMMERCIAL-PO-04 — ₹1,499 renewal.** A successful ₹1,499 renewal extends the ₹1,499 complete-package subscription for its next 30-day period; during that active period the customer retains access to ₹99 kit + ₹499 kit + ₹1,499 Client Finder. The renewal does NOT rewrite or replace any independently purchased ₹99/₹499 subscription; those continue on their own lifecycle unless separately cancelled/inactive.
>
> **COMMERCIAL-PO-05 — Retire old one-time ₹1,499 product.** The existing one-time product `ai_client_acquisition_1499` is NO LONGER the intended ₹1,499 commercial offering and must be treated as RETIRED FROM NEW PURCHASES. Its four static deliverables (acquisition-funnel PDF, delivery-workflow ZIP, automation-recipes ZIP, walkthrough MP4) are no longer the ₹1,499 commercial product offering. The ₹1,499 price point is now assigned to `ai_client_acquisition_1499_subscription` (the Client Finder subscription / complete package). Old files must NOT be deleted merely because the product is retired; database records must NOT be deleted; old records must NOT be automatically migrated.
>
> **COMMERCIAL-PO-06 — Development/test records.** The product is currently NOT LIVE. Existing records tied to the old one-time ₹1,499 product may be development/test/dummy records. For this governance task: do not assume they represent real customers, do not assume migration or deletion, do not create new entitlements from them, do not convert old one-time purchases into new subscriptions, do not perform cleanup. Instead, AUDIT ONLY — identify catalog/SKU definitions, orders, payments, entitlements, access records, tests/fixtures, and other references to the old SKU, and classify each as (A) production/customer data, (B) development/test/dummy data, or (C) unknown/requires verification, without altering any of it. If cleanup is appropriate, report it as a future engineering/data-cleanup action requiring separate authorization.
>
> **COMMERCIAL-PO-07 — Existing product functionality.** The intention is to unify the commercial/account/subscription architecture where appropriate, but NOT to silently change existing product functionality. ₹99 and ₹499 may become subscription-backed products, but their established functionality must not be changed merely because the subscription architecture is being unified. The new ₹1,499 Client Finder subscription introduces Client Finder functionality; no additional feature changes are to be inferred.

## D. SUBSCRIPTION / ENTITLEMENT IMPACT (explanatory only — not implemented)

- **₹99 and ₹499:** Today both are one-time purchases tracked only in the `entitlements` table (no expiry, no subscription-period concept, and — per `NINETY_NINE_KIT_FULFILLMENT_PRODUCT_OWNER_DECISION.md` — explicitly no refund/cancellation capability for ₹99). COMMERCIAL-PO-01/03 describe both as having their own 30-day subscription lifecycle with cancellation. Moving either onto that lifecycle is a product-and-architecture change the PO has now directed in principle, but has not yet specified in the detail engineering would need (e.g., does it reuse the existing `subscription_periods` table/schema the new ₹1,499 subscription uses, or something else; does "cancel" mean a refund-capable action or simply opting out of renewal).
- **₹1,499 (new subscription SKU):** Already modeled on `subscription_periods`, already architecturally capable of coexisting with independent ₹99/₹499 rows for the same user (no conflicting unique constraints). What is missing is the *complete-package access grant* itself — current subscription access-control code checks only its own slug and does not currently extend access to ₹99/₹499 the way the old one-time SKU's ladder logic does.
- **Independent subscriptions:** Schema-level support already exists (separate rows per `(user_id, product_slug)`, no exclusivity constraint). This is good news for COMMERCIAL-PO-03/04 — the data model does not need to change to allow coexistence; only the access-resolution logic for the ₹1,499 subscription needs to additionally check/grant ₹99+₹499.
- **Complete-package access:** Requires access-resolution logic (not yet existing) that, given an active ₹1,499 subscription row, also returns "has access" for `ai_income_99` and `ai_freelancing_499` — independent of whether the user separately holds entitlement/subscription rows for those SKUs.
- **Renewal:** Current webhook logic (`packages/core-payments/src/webhookHandler.ts`) inserts one new `subscription_periods` row per successful recurring charge and never mutates prior rows — this is already consistent with COMMERCIAL-PO-04 ("renewal... does NOT rewrite or replace" other subscriptions), since renewal of one product's row has no mechanical effect on another product's rows.
- **Cancellation:** No cancellation/refund webhook handling exists today beyond `refund.*` setting `refunded_at` on a `subscription_periods` row (per code-audit agent: "No webhook handlers for subscription lifecycle events (cancellation, failure, expiry)" — also previously recorded in this project's memory from the Oct 10 authentication-remediation audit). COMMERCIAL-PO-03's cancellation independence (Cases 3–5) is therefore currently untested by any existing mechanism for any of the three products, not just ₹99.
- **Entitlement calculation:** The `entitlements` table (one-time products) already supports multiple independent rows per customer. The gap is purely on the subscription side: today's single-slug check in `packages/core-subscriptions/src/access.ts` would need to become capable of (a) checking multiple product slugs per user and (b) expressing "this slug's access implies these other slugs' access too," mirroring what `ladder.ts` already does for one-time products.

No resolution or design is proposed here; this section only describes what the current architecture does and does not already provide.

## F. NEW OPEN PRODUCT OWNER QUESTIONS

1. **Scope of "retired from new purchases" (COMMERCIAL-PO-05).** Does this mean only that the checkout/catalog flow for `ai_client_acquisition_1499` should stop accepting new orders, while existing purchasers keep their current entitlement/download access unchanged — or something broader (e.g. removing it from public product listings as well)? This governance task was told not to infer additional feature changes, so this needs an explicit answer before any implementation is scoped.
2. **Does ₹99 need a true cancellation/refund capability?** `NINETY_NINE_KIT_FULFILLMENT_PRODUCT_OWNER_DECISION.md` explicitly decided "no refund or cancellation capability exists for the ₹99 product... unless a future, separate Product Owner decision changes this." COMMERCIAL-PO-03's Case 4 ("Customer cancels ₹99") appears to be exactly that future decision — but it is not certain whether "cancels" there means a customer-initiated refund/cancellation action, or simply non-renewal/natural 30-day expiry. Please confirm which.
3. **Is the `chavan.sharad89@gmail.com` order/payment/entitlement real or a self-test?** It is the one record found in this audit that does not look like fixture data. No action was taken on it (per COMMERCIAL-PO-06), but any future cleanup/retirement plan for the old SKU will need to know whether it must be preserved as a real customer record.
4. **Target architecture for ₹99/₹499 subscription lifecycle.** Should ₹99/₹499 move onto the same `subscription_periods` table/pattern the new ₹1,499 subscription already uses, or keep today's one-time `entitlements` table and simply add a 30-day expiry check to it? COMMERCIAL-PO-01 establishes that they must behave as subscriptions but does not specify which existing mechanism they should be built on.
