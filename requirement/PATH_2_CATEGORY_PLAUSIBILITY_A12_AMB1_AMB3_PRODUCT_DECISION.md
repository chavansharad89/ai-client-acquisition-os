# 3. AMB-1 — REJECT Grounds

### 3.1 Ambiguity

The A-12 decision requires a substantive **REJECT** finding to identify the uncited premise.

The Companion Record creation authorization additionally referred to an “other substantive reason.”

The question was whether that phrase permitted the Companion Record to create additional rejection categories beyond those already defined by F1-D §8.

### 3.2 Product Owner Decision

**Decision: NO.**

The Companion Record shall **not create additional REJECT categories** beyond the applicable rejection grounds already established in **F1-D §8**.

A REJECT finding shall:

1. correspond to an applicable F1-D §8 rejection ground;
2. identify the relevant substantive problem, including the uncited premise where applicable; and
3. provide the required grounding for the finding.

The phrase **“other substantive reason”** in the creation authorization shall not be interpreted as independent authority to invent a new rejection ground.

Where a case does not fit an applicable F1-D §8 rejection ground, the facilitator shall not manufacture a new category merely to force a REJECT.

The applicable existing outcome, including **NOT ASSESSABLE** where appropriate, shall be used instead.

### 3.3 Effect on Companion Record

The Companion Record's substantive-check logic shall therefore remain bounded by the existing F1-D §8 framework.

No change to F1-D §8 is authorized or required.

### 3.4 Status

**AMB-1: RESOLVED.**

# 4. AMB-3 — D11-H §22 Companion Reference

### 4.1 Ambiguity

The A-12 decision contemplated that the D11-H §22 session record would identify the Companion Record.

The Companion creation authorization simultaneously prohibited rewriting existing D11-H entries.

The question was whether a Companion reference could be entered into an existing D11-H §22 field during a future authorized validation session.

### 4.2 Product Owner Decision

**Decision: YES, prospectively, where an existing D11-H §22 field already provides the applicable place for the session record.**

During a future **separately authorized** validation session:

* an existing D11-H §22 field may be populated with the applicable Companion Record reference as part of normal session recording;
* this constitutes prospective session data entry;
* it does not constitute a D11-H schema, field, column, or template change.

The following remain prohibited:

* creating a new D11-H field for the Companion;
* adding a new D11-H column;
* modifying the D11-H template;
* renaming or repurposing an existing D11-H field;
* retroactively inserting a Companion reference into a completed D11-H record;
* rewriting an existing D11-H entry solely to accommodate the Companion.

The Companion Record remains the separate facilitator/analyst-side detailed §6.3 record.

### 4.3 Historical Records

No existing D11-H session record shall be retroactively modified under this ruling.

This decision applies only to future sessions for which the applicable validation activity has separately been authorized.

### 4.4 Status

**AMB-3: RESOLVED.**
